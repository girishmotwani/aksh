NEXT STEPS — do them in this order:

  1) Wait until the injector is Ready (its caBundle is patched only when Ready):

       kubectl -n {{ include "aksh-injector.namespace" . }} rollout status deploy/{{ include "aksh-injector.fullname" . }}

  2) ONLY THEN opt a workload namespace in (repeat for each namespace you want
     protected — your pods stay in their own namespaces):

       kubectl label namespace <your-namespace> {{ .Values.webhook.injectLabel.key }}={{ .Values.webhook.injectLabel.value }}

  3) Create (or restart) workloads in that namespace. The aksh-proxy sidecar is
     injected on pod CREATE — existing pods are not retro-injected:

       kubectl -n <your-namespace> rollout restart deploy/<app>

WHY ORDER MATTERS: failurePolicy is Fail. If you label a namespace before the
injector is Ready, pod creation there is blocked until it is. Never label the
injector's own namespace ({{ include "aksh-injector.namespace" . }}).
